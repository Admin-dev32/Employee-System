## Event Commission

Event commission is separate from the `$100/month` fixed payment.

Commission is earned only after a sale becomes valid.

A sale becomes valid when the client pays a deposit or pays in full.

Commission is calculated only on the main bar price before tax. Add-ons and extras are excluded from standard commission unless a future special bonus is specifically approved.
