## Base Pay

Note: The original PDF showed `$40/month`, but the current active amount is now `$100/month`.

This fixed monthly payment is for:

## Commission

Commission is paid per valid event sale.

A valid sale happens when the client pays a deposit or pays in full.

Commission is calculated only on the main bar value before tax.

Add-ons and extras do not enter the standard commission unless a future special bonus is approved.

### What Commission Pays For

Commission pays for:

- Closing sales and client attention
- Correct scheduling and operational preparation
- Pre-event support and instructions
- Related inventory control
- Post-event feedback, photo review, and quality review

| Stage 2 | 6 to 10 sales | 8% | 10% |

Sales above 15 stay in Stage 3 for now.

## When Payment Is Earned

Full commission is earned once the sale is valid, either by deposit or full payment.

## When Payment Is Paid

Full commission is paid once the sale is valid, either by deposit or full payment.

## Lead Ownership

- The main leader gets the commission.
- If there is a critical delay of 8 hours without client response and there is risk of losing the sale, the lead can be reassigned or overtaken.

## Payment Rule

- Full commission is paid once the sale is valid, either by deposit or full payment.
- If an administrative overpayment happens, adjustment is made from future commissions.

## Problems and Cancellations

- If the problem is company/system fault, there is no chargeback and the worker does not lose commission.
- If the problem is worker execution/service fault, there can be a partial or total chargeback decided by administration.
- Adjustments apply only to future commissions, not immediate repayment.

## Chargeback / Adjustment Rules

- Company/system fault: no chargeback; Fernando does not lose commission.
- Worker execution/service fault: administration may decide a partial or total chargeback.
- Administrative overpayments and adjustments are applied to future commissions only.
- No immediate repayment is required for adjustments.

## Approval Rules

- Only the supervisor can approve discounts.
- Fernando cannot invent promotions or discounts.
- Fernando cannot change prices from the official file.
- Fernando cannot promise exceptions to company policy.
- Fernando cannot offer refunds by personal decision.
- Fernando should ask for approval when needed.

## Review Cycle

Compensation and rules are reviewed every 2 months.
